## 📌 Project Overview

Amazon Prime Video hosts thousands of movies and TV shows from different countries, genres, and production houses. Understanding this content library is essential for making informed decisions regarding content acquisition, production strategy, recommendation systems, and audience engagement.

This project performs an end-to-end **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the Amazon Prime Video dataset by combining information from both **titles** and **credits** datasets. The analysis focuses on discovering meaningful patterns, identifying relationships between variables, and translating analytical findings into business recommendations.

---

**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**

**Contribution:** Individual Project

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Amazon Prime Video contains a diverse catalog of movies and TV shows across multiple genres, countries, and production years. Without proper analysis, it is difficult to understand content trends, audience preferences, and factors that contribute to highly rated content.

The goal of this project is to analyze the available data and generate insights that can support strategic business decisions related to content planning, licensing, marketing, and user engagement.

---

## 💼 Business Objectives

* Analyze the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
* Study content release trends over time.
* Identify the most common genres and production countries.
* Analyze runtime distribution across different content types.
* Evaluate IMDb and TMDb ratings.
* Explore relationships between runtime, ratings, popularity, and release year.
* Identify highly rated directors and content categories.
* Generate business recommendations based on analytical findings.

# 📈 Key Insights

### 🎥 Content Distribution

* Movies constitute the majority of Amazon Prime Video's content library.
* TV Shows represent a smaller but steadily growing segment.

### 📅 Release Trends

* The content catalog has expanded significantly in recent decades.
* Most available titles were released after 2000, indicating continuous platform growth.

### 🎭 Genre Analysis

* Drama and Documentary are among the most frequently occurring and highest-rated genres.
* Popular genres consistently attract higher audience engagement.

### 🌍 Production Countries

* The United States contributes the largest share of content.
* A relatively small number of countries dominate content production, highlighting opportunities for geographic diversification.

### ⭐ Audience Ratings

* IMDb and TMDb ratings show a positive correlation, suggesting consistency between different rating platforms.
* Most titles receive moderate to high audience ratings.

### 🎬 Directors

* Certain directors consistently produce highly rated content, demonstrating the value of experienced creative talent.

### 👥 Audience Engagement

* IMDb votes are concentrated among a relatively small number of highly visible titles.
* Popularity is driven by a combination of content quality, marketing, and audience reach.

# 📌 Executive Summary

The analysis reveals that Amazon Prime Video is primarily movie-focused, with a rapidly expanding content library. Drama and Documentary genres perform particularly well, while IMDb and TMDb ratings demonstrate strong agreement regarding content quality.

Although a few countries dominate production, there are clear opportunities for international expansion. Audience engagement is concentrated among a limited number of highly visible titles, and collaborations with consistently successful directors can further strengthen the platform's content strategy.

Overall, the project demonstrates how exploratory data analysis can transform raw streaming platform data into meaningful business intelligence that supports content acquisition, production planning, marketing, and recommendation systems.
